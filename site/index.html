SALKA

Adaptive traffic control for Irbid

Role
System design & research
Team
SYNTRA
Type
Self-initiated
Stack
Python, SUMO, TraCI, OpenStreetMap

Most traffic lights in Jordan run on fixed timers, blind to the queue in front of them. SALKA rebuilds Culture Circle as a digital twin in SUMO and retimes each green phase to the traffic that is actually there, with priority for emergency vehicles.

  1. ModelRoad network pulled from OpenStreetMap into SUMO.
  2. SimulatePeak-hour scenarios with cars, buses and congestion events.
  3. SenseQueues, waiting times and occupancy read live through TraCI.
  4. AdaptLonger greens for congested approaches. Ambulances first.
User layerCitizen app for routes, bus tracking and arrival times, plus an operator dashboard with congestion heatmaps.
Intelligence layerTraffic analysis, adaptive signal control, route optimization and data fusion.
Data layerVehicle counts, queue lengths, bus GPS, simulation output and emergency requests.

C-Layer

Autonomous CO₂ capture for heavy industry

Role
Sole designer, end to end
Status
Concept design and research. No prototype built yet.
Core
NVIDIA Jetson Nano, LSTM, NEMA 23 and 34 steppers, 316L stainless steel

Capture filters on cement and steel stacks saturate, and swapping them by hand means stopping the furnace. C-Layer is my design for a four-layer capture system where a robot replaces each saturated filter while the plant keeps running.

  1. Pre-filtrationStrips dust and large particles.
  2. Absorption IMEA bonded to multi-walled carbon nanotubes.
  3. Absorption IIA second MEA and CNT stage.
  4. SupportHolds the stack against heat and pressure.

Design targets

  • ~20 sRobotic filter swap, planned with no plant shutdown
  • 870°CStack heat handled with reflective shields, aerogel and vortex-tube cooling
  • RULAn LSTM on the Jetson predicts remaining filter life from CO₂, pressure and heat
  • Fail-safeA spring-return bypass damper opens on power loss to protect the furnace
