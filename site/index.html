SALKA

Up to 15% less waiting

Adaptive traffic control for Irbid

Most traffic lights in Jordan run on fixed timers. SALKA rebuilds Culture Circle in Irbid as a digital twin in SUMO and retimes green phases to the real queues, with priority for emergency vehicles.

Role
System design & research
Team
SYNTRA
Type
Self-initiated
  • Python
  • SUMO
  • TraCI
  • OpenStreetMap
How It Works
  1. Model. Culture Circle’s road network pulled from OpenStreetMap into SUMO.
  2. Simulate. Peak-hour scenarios with cars, buses and congestion events.
  3. Sense. Queues, waiting times and occupancy read live through TraCI in Python.
  4. Adapt. Longer greens for congested approaches, and emergency vehicles go first.

Result: up to 15% lower average waiting time, measured in simulation.

C-Layer

Concept design

Autonomous CO2 capture for heavy industry

Swapping saturated capture filters on cement and steel stacks usually means stopping the furnace. I designed a four-layer system where a robot replaces each saturated filter while the plant keeps running.

Role
Sole designer
Status
No prototype built yet
Type
Self-initiated
  • NVIDIA Jetson Nano
  • MEA + CNT
  • NEMA 34
  • Modbus TCP
  • MQTT
Design Targets
  • ~45 s filter swap. Planned with no plant shutdown.
  • Four layers. Pre-filtration, MEA and carbon-nanotube absorption stages, and a structural support layer.
  • Waste heat regeneration. Saturated filters release their CO2 in an on-site chamber heated by the plant, targeting 1.4-2.5 MJ per kg.
  • Predictive. Statistical degradation models on the Jetson first, then LSTM prediction of remaining filter life once data exists.
  • Built for 870°C. Reflective shields, aerogel insulation and vortex-tube cooling.
  • Fail-safe. A spring-return bypass damper opens on power loss to protect the furnace.
