SALKA

Adaptive traffic management for Irbid

My role
System design and research
Team
SYNTRA
Type
Self-initiated project
Tools
Python, SUMO, TraCI, OpenStreetMap

Traffic lights in Jordan mostly run on fixed schedules, whatever the real demand. SALKA rebuilds Culture Circle in Irbid as a digital twin and lets signal timing respond to the traffic that is actually there.

  1. Digital twinCulture Circle road network extracted from OpenStreetMap and imported into SUMO.
  2. ScenariosPeak-hour demand with private cars, public buses and congestion events.
  3. SensingQueue lengths, waiting times and occupancy read live through TraCI in Python.
  4. ControlGreen phases extended for congested approaches, with priority for emergency vehicles.

User layer

Citizen app for routes, bus tracking and arrival times. Operator dashboard with congestion heatmaps and signal status.

Intelligence layer

Traffic analysis, adaptive signal control, route optimization and data fusion into one view of the network.

Data layer

Vehicle counts, queue lengths, bus GPS positions, simulation output and emergency priority requests.

C-Layer

Autonomous CO₂ capture for factory stacks

Carbon capture on cement, steel and power plants usually needs manual filter changes. Each change can stop production and push backpressure into the furnace. I designed a system that swaps saturated filters with a robot while the plant keeps running.

My role
Sole designer, end to end
Status
Concept design and research. No physical prototype yet.
Core
NVIDIA Jetson Nano, LSTM, NEMA 23 and 34 steppers, 316L stainless steel
  1. Pre-filtrationStrips dust and large particles first.
  2. Chemical absorptionMEA bonded to multi-walled carbon nanotubes.
  3. Chemical absorptionA second MEA and CNT stage for remaining CO₂.
  4. Structural supportHolds the stack against heat and airflow pressure.

Design targets

  • ~20 srobotic filter swap, planned with no plant shutdown
  • 870°Cstack environment, handled with reflective shields, aerogel and vortex-tube cooling
  • RULLSTM model on the Jetson predicts remaining filter life from CO₂, pressure and heat
  • Fail-safespring-return bypass damper opens on power loss to protect the furnace
